# 🌿 AI-Driven Product Strategy & Automated Market Intelligence
**Google AI Essentials Certification | Lab Project**

## 📌 Project Overview
This project demonstrates the use of **Google Gemini** to orchestrate a complete product development workflow—from persona-based brainstorming to automated competitive intelligence. Using a **Green Tech** case study, I developed a strategy for a *Solar-Powered Modular Home Office Hub* by leveraging prompt engineering and autonomous AI actions.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills
* **AI Model:** Google Gemini (2026 Edition)
* **Skills:** Prompt Engineering (Persona/Task/Format), Data Synthesis, Cost-Benefit Analysis, AI Automation.
* **Features Used:** Scheduled Actions, Table Formatting, Real-time Trend Retrieval.

---

## 🚀 The Workflow

### Phase 1: Contextual Brainstorming
I assigned Gemini the persona of a **Sustainability Consultant** to generate eco-conscious accessories for a modular home office.
* **Key Prompt:** *"You are a Sustainability Consultant in the Green Tech industry..."*
* **Top Concept:** **AI Smart-Power Strip** (Automated phantom-load reduction).

### Phase 2: Strategic Analysis (Decision Matrix)
To move from ideation to execution, I prompted Gemini to synthesize the raw ideas into a structured decision matrix.

| Accessory Idea | Manufacturing Cost | Sustainability Benefit | Consumer Appeal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AI Smart-Power Strip** | Low | High | High |
| **Ocean-Plastic Organizers** | Low | Medium | High |
| **Hydroponic Planter** | Medium | Medium | Very High |
| **Kinetic Energy Mats** | High | Low | Medium |

> **Insight:** The analysis identified the **AI Smart-Power Strip** as the "Quick Win" for high impact at a low entry cost.

### Phase 3: 2026 Industry Trend Research
I queried the model for real-time 2026 trends to ensure the product strategy aligned with future regulations and consumer behavior.
* **Trend 1:** "Circular Economy" demand (Modular/Repairable parts).
* **Trend 2:** "Profit-First AI" (Tech that actively reduces utility bills).

### Phase 4: Workflow Automation
To eliminate manual research overhead, I utilized Gemini's **Scheduled Actions** to automate the trend analysis.
* **Action:** Configured the analysis to run **weekly**.
* **Result:** Created a persistent intelligence loop that keeps the product roadmap updated without manual intervention.

## 📊 Impact & Key Takeaways
* **Efficiency:** Reduced initial market research time by approximately 80%.
* **Precision:** Converted unstructured creative ideas into an executive-ready comparison table.
* **Future-Proofing:** Implemented autonomous monitoring to capture shifting 2026 Green Tech regulations.
